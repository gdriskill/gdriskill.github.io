Chess

This project is a group final project for the Object-Oriented Programming and Design course (CSC 335) at the University of Arizona in Fall 2022. My group created a chess game that included networked and local multiplayer, an AI opponent, and GUI with sound effects and animations. Throughout the project, my group practiced an agile development framework by organizing work into weeklong sprints, communicating daily with team members, and aiming for iterative and incremental development.

I was personally responsible for creating the game's model, which included the piece movement logic, checking for checkmates and checks, and other game rules, and the half of the classes for individual game pieces. I applied object-oriented principles and common design patterns to write code that was easily combined with code from other group members.
